The D.I.L.™ framework

A practical path from insight to lasting improvement.

My approach connects operational diagnosis with focused improvement and people leadership—so organisations can turn findings into action and build the capability to sustain results.

D

Diagnose

Understand the current state, identify performance gaps and uncover the root causes behind operational challenges.

I

Improve

Prioritise practical solutions, strengthen processes and track meaningful improvements in quality, delivery and productivity.

L

Build leadership capability, engage teams and embed the habits that help improvements last beyond the initial project.

Experience with purpose

Three decades of turning operational challenges into results.

I am an operations and Lean transformation professional with more than 30 years of experience in manufacturing, regional operations leadership, people management and continuous improvement. My career includes leadership roles with Schneider Electric and Maccaferri, working across diverse teams, sites and operational environments.

I bring a practical, people-centred approach to improvement: understand the real problem, engage the people closest to the work, and build systems and leadership habits that make better performance sustainable.

My work has included Lean Manufacturing and operational excellence initiatives that delivered measurable improvements in lead time, productivity, inventory, space utilisation and cost savings.
